# Swing pricing status of FTN losers and 2019-purge funds: notes and sources

Collected 11-12 Oct 2026. Files in this folder:

- `swing_status_ftn_losers.csv`: 78 losers (role = loser) from `agentA_task1_round_membership.csv`, one row each.
- `swing_status_2019_purge.csv`: the 60 largest funds (by max mv_tot in 2018-19) whose last observation in `ppm_panel_2018_2026.csv` falls between 2018-11-30 and 2019-12-31.
- `build_outputs.py`: the script that produced both CSVs (all fund-level findings are hard-coded there with source URLs).
- `summary.json`: counts by round.

Status conventions. `swing_status_at_exit` is the fund's status on its `avslutsdatum` (or current status for the 2026-05-28 round, which has no exits yet). YES = the fund rules / prospectus in force allowed an adjusted NAV (justerat fondandelsvarde, swing pricing, swing factor, svingprising). NO = they did not. UNKNOWN = not established. `verification`: VERIFIED = read in fondbestammelser, prospectus, annual/half-year report or the fund company's unitholder notice of an FI-approved rule change; SECONDARY = company web page, news, or inference from a complete list of rule changes; UNKNOWN = nothing usable found. `evidence_doc_date` shows when the document read was dated, so the reader can see when the evidence post-dates the exit (flagged in notes). `applied_disclosed` is only filled where a report was actually read; N/A means the fund does not apply the tool.

## 1. Legal background (verified)

- SFS 2023:234 (issued 4 May 2023) inserted 4 kap. 10 b in lagen (2004:46) om vardepappersfonder: "Ett fondbolag far efter tillstand av Finansinspektionen tillampa ett justerat fondandelsvarde for att motverka att de befintliga eller kvarvarande fondandelsagarna paverkas negativt av transaktionskostnader som uppstar for en vardepappersfond vid betydande nettofloden in i eller ut ur fonden." In force 1 July 2023. Source: https://svenskforfattningssamling.se/sites/default/files/sfs/2023-05/SFS2023-234.pdf (prop. 2022/23:65 "Ett likviditetsverktyg for fonder").
- FI beslutspromemoria FI dnr 22-25593 (decided 2023-06-20, FFFS 2023:10 and 2023:11 amending FFFS 2013:9 and 2013:10, in force 1 July 2023): the FI permission is granted through FI's approval of the fund rules (there is no separate permission register); 23 kap. 12 a requires the fund rules to describe the method, the threshold and the highest justeringsfaktor; 29 kap. 17 p. 6 requires the informationsbroschyr to describe the tool, the highest factor and a calculation example; 31 kap. 48 b (via 34 and 56) requires the annual and half-year report to state the method and the justeringsfaktor actually used in the period (the threshold need not be disclosed); 7 kap. 8 a requires board-approved routines. Source: https://www.fi.se/globalassets/media/dokument/fffs-bilagor/2023/beslutspm-fs2310-2311.pdf
- Consequence for data collection: the only primary evidence is (a) the fund's fondbestammelser or informationsbroschyr, (b) the company's unitholder notice of the FI-approved rule change, (c) the annual/half-year report. Swedish funds cannot have applied the tool before 1 July 2023 (this settles the 2019 purge for Swedish funds).
- Pensionsmyndigheten's remissvar asking for the tool to be mandatory was not re-read in this run (it is in the regeringen.se remiss bundle for "Ett likviditetsverktyg for fonder": https://www.regeringen.se/contentassets/79c71613e58f40a4aaf08852a3b6c371/ett-likviditetsverktyg-for-fonder.pdf).

## 3. FI permission list

There is no FI register or press-release series of permissions to use justerat fondandelsvarde: per FI's beslutspromemoria the permission is embedded in FI's approval of the amended fondbestammelser (4 kap. 9 LVF), so the trail is the fund company's unitholder notice and the dated fondbestammelser. The company adoption dates found are: Lannebo (fixed-income funds) 11 Dec 2023 (board adoption; FI approval date not stated); Nordea Funds AB Sweden before 30 May 2024 (partial adjustment from that date); SEB 14 March 2025; Storebrand Fonder 1 Sept 2025 / 12 Nov 2025; Carnegie (four funds, date not found). No adoption found for Avanza, Spiltan, Aktie-Ansvar, PriorNilsson, Pareto, Simplicity, Handelsbanken, Lansforsakringar, Indecap. Unknown for Swedbank Robur, Skandia, Cicero, Humle/Atle.

## 5. 2019 purge CSV

Selection: funds whose last panel observation is between 2018-11-30 and 2019-12-31, ranked by max mv_tot over 2018-19, top 60 (352 funds left in that window). Domicile from the ISIN prefix (XS ISINs are the C WorldWide Luxembourg SICAV 1A classes). The window also catches non-purge exits (e.g. Swedbank Robur MEGA share classes and SEB Generationsfonder that were merged in 2019, C WorldWide 1A classes replaced by new classes), which are not separated here; the reason for exit is not classified in the CSV. 38 of the 60 are Swedish (swing impossible before July 2023, VERIFIED by law), 4 Finnish (Alandsbanken 2 and Nordea 1 marked NO on the basis of the companies' later adoption dates; Ålandsbanken Premium 100 is by far the largest), 17 Luxembourg and 1 Irish (UNKNOWN; 2019 prospectuses not checked).

## 6. What could not be verified and why

- Tooling: the shared web-search budget ran out roughly a third of the way through, Firecrawl had no credits, and direct downloads (curl) are blocked by the egress policy, so everything after that point relied on fetching guessed or page-discovered URLs. Long PDFs were truncated at roughly 90-140k characters of text, which hid the general NAV chapters of Danske Invest, C WorldWide (max factor) and Handelsbanken's Oct 2025 omnibus letter, and the fund sections of several annual reports.
- Swedish gaps: Swedbank Robur Nordenfond (documents on internetbank.swedbank.se), Skandia Sverige Exponering and Smabolag Sverige (JS-only site), Aktiespararna Direktavkastning (Cicero), Humle Smabolagsfond (robots block), LF Europa Index and Global Index (unreadable Nov 2024 letter), and the fondbestammelser themselves for LF, Handelsbanken, Indecap and the ex-Ohman funds (status inferred from complete rule-change lists).
- `applied_disclosed` (FFFS 31 kap. 48 b): only established for Nordea Institutionella Foretagsobligationsfonden (not a loser). For the Swedish YES funds (SEB Sverige Expanderad, Storebrand Sverige and Sverige Plus, Nordea Swedish Stars) the 2025 annual reports were not read; SEB's 2025 arsberattelse, Storebrand's 2025 arsberattelse and Nordea's Swedish Stars section (p. 572 of the Swedish-funds annual report 2025) are the documents to read next.
- Foreign UCITS: 19 Luxembourg funds UNKNOWN (BlackRock x2, GS x3, abrdn x2, Danske x4, BL x2, Fidelity, Pictet, Franklin Templeton, Allianz, CB European Quality, SEB Lux). A follow-up with working search should retrieve the prospectus in force at each exit date and record the swing-pricing clause and cap (typically 2%, with 3% exceptions in some platforms).
- Adoption dates for the Norwegian managers (Holberg, DNB, Storebrand/Delphi) and for Nordea Swedish Stars are missing; Norwegian svingprising follows the VFF industry standard and in several cases pre-dates the PPM exits by years, but this was not documented here.
