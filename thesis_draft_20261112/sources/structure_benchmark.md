# Course requirements and prior thesis structure

The revised draft follows the six-section research-paper structure in Riccardo Sabbatucci's BE451 introductory lecture. His original course documents are the direct evidence of his requirements. The selected earlier theses were examined by Adrien d'Avernas and inform presentation, not a claim about Sabbatucci's private preferences or future prize outcomes. This note preserves the earlier structural review and incorporates the full-text diligence of 12 November 2026; the detailed comparison appears in [course_winner_blueprint.md](course_winner_blueprint.md).

## Controlling course documents

The original Autumn 2026 syllabus (version 14 August 2026) and 27 introductory slides are pinned in the [source repository](https://github.com/uvangludvig-ship-it/financethesis/tree/d2d41666ed213e4cc1f6cb382313f59c29e87ea0/reference/course_context/source_materials). The originating authenticated source is [SSE Canvas, BE451 course 2864](https://sse.instructure.com/courses/2864). The review read those archived original documents; it does not assert that the live Canvas course has remained unchanged.

- Maximum 40 single-spaced pages in 12-point type, excluding appendices.
- Main text approximately 25–40 pages from introduction to conclusion, including the most relevant exhibits.
- Use the official front-page template for submission.
- Follow an empirical Journal of Finance style; no table of contents.
- A well-defined question, a focused comparison to nearest research, critical data discussion, empirical analysis and a concise conclusion.
- A replication must add a meaningful extension; transferring a study to a Nordic market is not automatically sufficient (introductory slide 10).
- Use simple methods unless added complexity is justified (slide 13); replicate the nearest paper's main quantitative exercise first (slide 17).
- Disclose AI assistance. AI directly writing thesis text is prohibited by the course rule. The present AI-generated working draft is not a compliant submission version.

Slide 19 gives the controlling six-section sequence:

1. Introduction: 2–4 pages, covering motivation, question, methodology, results and interpretation.
2. Related Literature: less than one page, focused on the closest studies and the contribution.
3. Data and Methodology.
4. Main Empirical Analysis.
5. Interpretation of Empirical Findings / Discussion.
6. Conclusion.

The current manuscript calls the fourth section “Empirical results” and the fifth “Discussion”; their functions match this sequence. Secondary mathematical and inferential details belong in self-contained appendices (syllabus p. 6). Slide 18 specifically discourages textbook explanations, exhaustive literature reviews and student-project digressions. The page and formatting rules are in syllabus pp. 5–6; the AI rule is on p. 10.

The course's direct instructions control where an old winner differs. Longer text or more regressions do not themselves meet the quality criteria better.

## Application to this draft

The introduction gives the question, sample, result magnitudes and causal boundary. The related-literature section is focused. Data and methodology explain the Swedish adaptation before the main return results. Demand measurement, anticipation and trading evidence then develop the economic argument. Discussion and conclusion answer the starting question. The core uses self-contained tables with units, windows, counts and inference methods; appendices preserve supplementary sensitivity and provenance.

Greenwood and Sammon control the economic sequence rather than the old theses. The published comparison uses additions **below 1%**, not an unverified precise 0.3%, and deletions of **+0.1%** in 2010–2020, as stated in the [published abstract](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13410). Detailed methods are checked against the [March 2024 author manuscript](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/2cc676bd-c8da-40e7-b112-3ecc8644e687), cited separately as `greenwood2024ms`. Its 2025 repository cover does not authenticate it as the final published text, and its abstract/body numbers differ. The full Version of Record and official replication code were not recovered. [The replication map](greenwood_sammon_replication_map.md) records these limits and the Swedish departures.

No empirical findings or prose are copied from the examples. The structure is an editorial judgment applied to this dataset, not a replication of an examiner-specific template.
