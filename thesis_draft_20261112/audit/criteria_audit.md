## 4. Final editorial priorities

1. **Use Claude's argumentative clarity, not its unresolved claims.** The useful story is the US puzzle, the Swedish residual, the demand/trading evidence, and the question of what the residual represents. Avoid an abstract dominated by checks or a title asserting disappearance was rejected in every recent subperiod.
2. **Keep one coherent evidence hierarchy.** Main return pattern first; matched composition/anticipation and demand/trading next; calendar and fixed-cohort extensions as interpretation; twin comparison as an informative but limited within-firm test. The calendar result is valuable, but an ex post seven-day decomposition does not by itself explain the historical decline.
3. **Use the later decade for the principal positive statement.** State 2.15% [1.01%, 3.39%] for 2017–2026 and separately state that the latest 1.50% estimate is imprecise and sensitive. Do not conflate “significant pooled recent association” with “no further decline.”
4. **Explain what the quantity measures buy.** The ratio 2.63 describes 2.71% mean CAR divided by 1.03% measured purchases on 136 additions. FI's matched check supports the recent scale; it does not repair historical coverage. That is useful economic evidence without a causal elasticity claim.
5. **End on the economic answer.** Positive recent addition returns coexist with benchmark-linked purchases and concentrated implementation trading; their timing and shared movement across share classes constrain, but do not identify, the mechanism. This meets the course's demand for interpretation more effectively than concluding with a catalogue of inference limitations.

## Implementation in the final narrative revision

The final draft uses a self-contained anchor-first introduction and the six required sections. Every empirical extension now appears in Results; Discussion interprets that evidence rather than introducing an additional test. The primary positive statement remains the pooled later-decade addition association, with the uncertain latest period stated next to it. The timing decomposition is supporting measurement evidence, not a causal explanation or a new confirmatory headline.

Claude's useful questions about the remaining return are retained: comparison securities, the post-implementation path and sister classes. Its universal migration assertion, sevenfold cross-country impact comparison, lower-bound demand claim and causal twin bound are removed. The fund-flow presentation and R-squared claims were independently verified before inclusion. Sources of structural inspiration and related Nordic research are attributed. The long mapping and technical uncertainty checks remain in appendices and the support package.

Render counts and layout checks are recorded in the final document-validation file, rather than inferred from old winners. Outstanding submission requirements remain author-written prose and accurate AI disclosure, verified student details on the official cover, complete historical source/report reconciliation where required, and authorized delivery of the full data/code package. Presentation, opposition and independent command of the analysis are author responsibilities that a written draft cannot establish. No prize probability or guaranteed compliance certification is made.
