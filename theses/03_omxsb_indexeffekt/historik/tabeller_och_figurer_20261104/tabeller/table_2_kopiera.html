Market value: mcap_pre in SEK billion, measured before AD; this source field is company market value, whereas Size uses share-class value at AD−1 as a percentage of pre-review OMXSB value. Turnover: mean daily SEK turnover divided by share-class value over the 21 trading days before AD, less the value-weighted main-list mean, in percentage points. Residual volatility: square root of residual variance from daily percentage returns on OMXSBGI over AD−250 to AD−2 (at least 100 observations; Wurgler and Zhuravskaya, 2002), in percent per day. Analysts: FY1 EPS forecast count in the month before AD. Run-up: summed daily excess returns over OMXSBGI over AD−100 to AD−1, in percent.
