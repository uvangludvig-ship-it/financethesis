PROPOSED, NOT AUTHORIZATION TO EXECUTE OUTCOMES
2026-11-04. Exact conceptual specification for root review.

Fixed population: 70 original additions before 2024, 10 reviews; event and
the same c1/c2/c3. Retain original fund IDs and q1 component=new status from
the validated old ledger. No q2-dependent cohort selection or replacement.
There are 737 event entrants and 639 peer-role entrants (211/188/240).

Let r index event or control role and f an original entrant (q0=0,q1>0).
Let a_r = 100 * P_r0 / (Mcap_r0 * f01_r).
Let w_rf = a_r * Q_rf1 > 0 be original size in q0 company-value pp.
This differs from the original F17 ledger's q1 valuation. Both q0 price and
company capitalization must pass the inherited source/snapshot/unit gate.
q0 shares used in a class-fraction diagnostic are NOT the denominator.
f01 and f12 are sourced physical split factors, not price-ratio estimates.

Known q2 support mask remains EXACTLY the old F35 event-set pass AND fund
in its ELIGIBLE_FUNDS. A new q0-only repair, if approved, may supply an
unknown cell's upper capacity but MUST NOT silently upgrade the q2 mask.
For a supported valid q2 cell:
  c_rf = min(1, Q_rf2 / (f12_r * Q_rf1)).
  l_rf = u_rf = w_rf * c_rf.
For an unsupported/invalid q2 cell:
  l_rf = 0; u_rf = w_rf.
Any original cohort/q0/q1 error is fatal; it is not q2 missingness.
q2 absence means zero only under the accepted nonempty-report, stable-fund
and instrument-identity rules. Missing report is unknown, not zero.

Role interval:
  L_r = sum_f l_rf; U_r = sum_f u_rf.
For a genuinely empty original entrant cohort, L_r=U_r=0, including when
the role's q0 scale or q2 source is unavailable. No 0/0 ratio is defined.
For a nonempty cohort an invalid q0 scale prevents finite certified bounds;
do not substitute q1 pp or drop the event.

Primary event contrast interval:
  L_i = L_event - (U_c1+U_c2+U_c3)/3
  U_i = U_event - (L_c1+L_c2+L_c3)/3.
Average bounds equally over events within review, then equally over the
10 reviews. Report all 70 event and 10 review bounds, plus the primary pair.
These are conservative source-missingness bounds for the observed sample,
not confidence intervals. Shared raw positions can make them non-sharp.
No bootstrap, p-value, t-interval, regression, subgroup or new peer outcome
family is part of this proposal.

One primary question: is there a positive capped endpoint level relative to
the original three peers at q2, on all 70 events and the original funds?
Lower>0: positive observed relative level despite allowed missing values.
Upper<0: negative observed relative level under the same assumptions.
Otherwise: sign unresolved. Exact zero is not affirmative equivalence.
No result identifies a causal index effect or higher persistence rate.

Baseline-only diagnostic that must precede opening new q2 quantities:
  B_r = sum_f w_rf
  M_r = sum_f w_rf * I(original q2 support unknown)
  source width_i = M_event + (M_c1+M_c2+M_c3)/3.
Mean with the identical event/review weights. This width is determined by
q1 sizes and the frozen support mask, independently of q2 outcomes. Report
event and peer unknown masses separately plus the baseline contrast
  B_i = B_event - mean(B_peers).
Do not multiply the old average 83–97% fraction by a capital mean: averaging
ratios and valuation changes do not commute. Nor may the old F35 59-event
baseline be substituted for the new all70 baseline.
