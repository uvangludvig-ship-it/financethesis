Scaled OMXSB auction study — 2026-11-03, fixed before new broad auction outcomes.
Technical design note, not thesis prose. Exploratory: prior daily analyses and the two2023 pilot contrasts are known. This is not blind preregistration.

ONE ECONOMIC QUESTION
On the implementation session, do implemented OMXSB additions/deletions exhibit an extra direction-signed price step in the closing auction relative to fixed same-session peers and the same securities' fixed comparison session? This locates a price difference at execution time. It does not identify tracking-fund orders, actual implementation shortfall, the entire announcement-to-implementation cost, or a causal OMXSB mechanism.

ONE PRIMARY ESTIMAND
Let d1 be the trading session ED−1 and d0 be ED−6. ED is the first effective index session. Use the fixed calendar, never an individual stock's nonmissing-price rows. For stock j and date d:
AP(j,d)=10000*(Pauction(j,d)/Mpre(j,d)-1), basis points.
Pauction is the final closing uncross price in documented unadjusted same-share SEK units (currently LSEG CLS_AUC). Mpre is the last unambiguous positive uncrossed displayed best-bid/ask midpoint with positive sizes in the final60seconds strictly before the historically valid closing-call start. The validated low-volume export must reproduce this same last quote; first-minute/mean-minute/VWAP is not an alternative estimand.
For event i, three fixed controls c1,c2,c3 and sign s=+1 addition/−1 deletion:
D_i=s*{[AP(i,d1)−(AP(c1,d1)+AP(c2,d1)+AP(c3,d1))/3]−[AP(i,d0)−(AP(c1,d0)+AP(c2,d0)+AP(c3,d0))/3]}.
All eight stock-date cells must pass. Same three controls and equal weights on both dates. Do not compute a two-control version or replace an unavailable control. First average D_i across all eligible events in review r; then average the review means with equal review weights. The within-review addition/deletion mixture follows the actual eligible event counts; it is not forced to50/50. The estimand is an average review's covered event, not aggregate SEK cost.

TWO SUBORDINATE CONTRASTS ONLY
Repeat the identical D_i and equal-review procedure separately for additions and deletions. No additional window, volume share, ownership/demand interaction, overnight reversal, liquidity split, era split, threshold search or alternative benchmark is part of this execution. Those would require separate justification and a new declaration rather than expansion after results.

CONTROLS AND CONFOUNDING
Use the already frozen dated-main-list eligible_rematch sets for2017+ where available. They were chosen from preAD log market capitalization and preAD market-adjusted return history, with three distinct issuers and current/previous review events excluded. Do not mislabel these as liquidity-matched controls. Older T2 sets are metadata leads only until their dated instrument/listing identity is verified; no replacement after source availability or outcomes.
The controls are dated listed peers without a known contemporaneous announced membership change, not certified nonmembers, unaffected securities or zero-tracker-demand stocks. Existing membership flags remain descriptive; 'not_in_chain' or absent history is unknown. At the issuer level, exclude a whole event/control set if a control is a current OMXSB event or a known cancelled/reversed announcement; do not replace it. Before broad outcomes, freeze a source-verified other-index event register for the two target sessions and the interval between them. Apply confirmed MSCI/OMXS30/other-index overlap exclusions symmetrically to event and control roles. Existing manually transcribed MSCI rows are candidate leads, not a verified exclusion list; resolve candidate class/date/side from primary lists, retain unresolved status. Unknown other-index coverage prevents an OMXSB-specific causal interpretation and must not be silently called clean.
Month-end/global-index trading is precisely why same-date peers are essential. Even these controls can themselves rebalance, differ in liquidity or face correlated news. Paired differences remain observational. ED−6 can lie before, on or after AD: report this by review, never move the date. If after AD, the contrast measures additional implementation-session dislocation, not the whole announcement response. The known2023H2 pilot review remains identified in the full sample; no claim that its unseen peers or the expanded sample are blind holdouts.

SOURCE GATES
Fixed event/role/RIC/dated ISIN/listing/currency; final implemented status; exact trading days; historical auction phase schedule. Use the prior explicitly amended two-candidate clock method: local exchange clock versus UTC, validated against documented opening and continuous phases. Do not search arbitrary offsets. Label clock inference honestly.
Actual source rows and returned dates, unique keys, positive auction/quote values, units, timestamp ordering and conflicting final-quote ties must pass. Genuine auction field only; no last-trade/official-close fallback. SHoF missing closing rows do not stop a price-only design when a separately validated CLS_AUC exists. Unresolved transaction double counting prevents order-flow/volume claims but does not invent a price failure. For simple AP all price inputs are within the same day, so a split between d0 and d1 is not itself a scale problem; record corporate actions/news and block any within-session identity/unit break. Do not infer absence from the incomplete corporate-action register. No cross-day raw return is part of this design.
Full reconstruction versus lower-volume export validation is frozen separately in SAMPLING_VALIDATION_SPEC.txt. If equivalence or freshness cannot be established, full-book/same-measure source acquisition is required; do not substitute an aggregated-price estimator.

INFERENCE AND REPORTING
Show every review mean and count, every exclusion reason, the actual number of reviews G, addition/deletion composition, repeated issuer/control participation and calendar overlap. Report the primary equal-review mean with a95% Student-t interval based on the sample SD of review means divided by sqrt(G), dfG−1; no interval with G<2. This interval is model-based and conditional on sufficiently independent review averages. Review aggregation permits arbitrary within-review dependence, not serial dependence from repeated issuers, reused controls or persistent market states. With around8reviews it is not a dependence-robust decisive test. No causal/randomization p-value and no power or detectable-effect promise before actual source support.
For the two subgroup contrasts use97.5% marginal t intervals (Bonferroni at least95% simultaneous coverage for this pair under the same assumptions), clearly separate from the primary95% interval. This does not repair the entire project's earlier specification search. Do not pick clustering, windows, outlier trimming or weighting after results. Missing reviews change the observed target population and are reported; coverage never gives a retained review extra weight. No winsorization based on outcomes. The result and uncertainty are useful whether positive, negative or imprecise, but imprecision is not evidence of no economically meaningful effect.
