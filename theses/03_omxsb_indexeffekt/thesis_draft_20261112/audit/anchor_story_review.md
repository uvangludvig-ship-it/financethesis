# Anchor and literature review of the narrative draft

Prepared 12 November 2026. Reviewed `claude_draft_text.txt` against the previously inspected original research texts, the frozen Swedish implementation and the subsequent independent diligence. Paragraph identifiers below refer to that text. The attached draft is evidence to evaluate, not authority for method or source claims. No final manuscript, data or production code changed.

## Recommended story

Keep the draft's direct question, anchor-first opening, numerical answer and progression from returns to trading to interpretation. The defensible conclusion is **a positive later-decade addition association, with uncertain latest-period magnitude and an unresolved mechanism**. Use the title *The Index Effect in Sweden* and the question “Has the index effect disappeared, and what remains?” Avoid the categorical *The Index Effect That Did Not Disappear*: it suppresses the latest-block uncertainty and invites a permanent/causal reading.

The strongest empirical sequence is baseline returns → timing/composition/anticipation → measured fund trading → liquidity and concurrent index activity → remaining-return comparisons and share classes. The timing decomposition is a useful extension and a qualification of the historical comparison, not a replacement headline chosen because a new window is significant. Holdings and twins are economic evidence with specific limits, rather than a general catalogue of reasons the paper cannot say anything.

## High-priority substantive corrections

1. **Production classification, P013/P042–045 and exhibit notes.** The production demand uses curated, dated B/V/C classifications. The separate index-trade diagnostic does not mechanically generate those classifications. Their evidentiary basis may combine fund documents and observed trading; the code does not justify “we identify trackers exactly as they do” or “defined by trading behaviour, not mandate.” Describe actual dated classification and the distinct diagnostic. Do not turn this into a contribution claiming that the original 50% rule was replicated unless that chain is independently demonstrated.
2. **Demand is covered, not a mathematical lower bound (P020/P052/P235).** Missing investors may buy or sell; broad included funds can trade for reasons unrelated to membership; missing-position imputations can err in either direction. Incomplete coverage does not prove that aggregate net index demand exceeds observed net purchases. Consequently, 2.63 is not an upper bound on a true structural multiplier.
3. **Adjustment screen, P049.** The code compares an adjustment proxy at ED and the final available price date against a tolerance band. It does not establish that every excluded stock actually split after the event. Use “share-adjustment consistency screen”; explain its historical attrition. The draft's warning that the old exhibit notes say “between reports” is useful, but its replacement claim is also too strong.
4. **Source verification, P013/P033.** The register has 536 entries and source links; this review has not authenticated every field from original notices. Do not say every event is verified from a primary notice. A linked record and a completed field-level reconciliation are different.
5. **Exact replication and return conventions, P023/P037/P038.** Use replication and adaptation of the empirical framework, not “table by table.” Arithmetic daily excess sums and their inclusive first-return semantics are explicit Swedish choices; exact original program equivalence remains unresolved. The deletion screen is not authenticated as the original symmetric 100-day acquisition/listing rule.
6. **Composition, P016/P238.** “The added firms have not changed in ways that matter” is not an estimated result. Say the selected observable controls do not eliminate the earliest-to-latest difference on the matched sample. Residual selection remains.
7. **Comparison stocks, P017/P205.** Replace the old 1.53-point result with the repaired AD−100 through AD−2 control: 1.65 points, analytical CR1 interval [0.48,2.83], separate restricted-bootstrap p=0.0114. Main-market comparison is 1.49, [0.24,2.74], p=0.0288. This remains conditional, not causal, and “not simply large stocks that have risen” should not imply all selection is removed.
8. **Reversal, P006/P163/P232/P239.** Failure to reject a negative post-event mean does not prove no reversal. Use the same-cohort nonoverlapping cumulative extension and its uncertainty; a positive cumulative return through ED+20 is not permanent impact and need not imply positive post-effective drift.
9. **Twins, P019/P219/P232/P235.** The 0.33-point mean and shared leg movements are useful. An upper confidence endpoint around 1.2 is not a causal upper bound on a class-specific mechanism. The placebo procedure draws events independently despite repeat firms and common review shocks; spillovers invalidate a simple untreated-sister interpretation. Do not claim identical cash-flow rights for every A/B pair without issuer-level verification. Use “share classes of the same issuer.”
10. **Mechanism literature, P232.** Wurgler–Zhuravskaya supports the importance of substitutability, not a direct proof that arbitrage transmits Swedish index buying equally across A/B classes. Greenwood (2005) is more directly relevant to spillovers through related securities and hedging, but it too is motivation, not identification here.
11. **M inference, P015/P056.** Preserve ratio-of-means units and same-event numerator/denominator. State 2.63 as a descriptive ratio. The audited Fieller interval [0.91,4.37] and inherited percentile interval measure uncertainty differently; do not silently select the narrower or US-excluding interval. Do not assert an estimated Swedish elasticity −1/M.
12. **MDE/inversion, P064/P065 and notes.** The t-quantile MDE is an analytical approximation, not an exact smallest detectable effect of the bootstrap or proof that every smaller effect escapes detection. The inherited finite-bound interval search does not guarantee the complete acceptance set; infinity at the numerical search boundary is not authenticated mathematical unboundedness.
