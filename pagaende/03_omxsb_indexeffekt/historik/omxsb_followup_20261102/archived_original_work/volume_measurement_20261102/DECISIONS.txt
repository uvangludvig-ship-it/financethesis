Exploratory measurement feasibility and description, 2026-11-02 09:39 UTC.
Motivation: Chinco and Sammon (JFE 2024) distinguish observed index funds from broader index-linked trading. This check asks whether existing OMXSB daily volumes and quarterly holdings support that measurement direction. It cannot identify passive ownership from daily volume alone.
Previously known: aggregate volume concentration and mean fund quantity changes. New associations between volume and the audited quantity panel have not been examined for this follow-up.

Freeze 216 events 2019-2025 from demand_quarter/events_locked.csv. Trading day is ED-1 on the Swedish index calendar. Primary baseline is mean daily volume AD-61 through AD-12, 50 sessions, at least40 valid nonnegative observations. Primary volume outcome is ED-1 volume minus baseline; negative excess is retained. No maximize-over-days operation. One stated sensitivity uses mean ED-22 through ED-2, 21 sessions, at least17 observations.
Use two fixed fund definitions from the existing demand audit: documented stable OMXSB family subset and all stable family candidates. Use absolute net quantity, not sum of positive/negative trades. Quarterly net changes are not daily trades. Exclude known split/conversion-factor events from this initial cross-source unit comparison; log them. Check panel close at after-quarter against the accepted FI class price, within2%, with calendar staleness <=5 days. If unit/venue coverage cannot be established, report a descriptive restricted sample and a failed passive-ownership identification gate, not inferred unobserved investors.
In passing rows report event and equal-review means of excess volume and observed position change in common after-quarter-price / company-marketcap units. Report ratios with joint Fieller intervals by review. For diagnostic association only, regress excess-volume percentage on absolute observed quantity percentage, review fixed effects, equal review weights, SE clustered by review, no return outcomes. It is not causal and not an estimate of hidden passive ownership. Add a predetermined lagged turnover control (baseline volume in same unit) as the one slope sensitivity. Both event types separately and pooled with type intercept. Keep all outcomes; no re-cutting on significance.
This feasibility does not replicate Chinco-Sammon's marketwide passive share: historical index weights, intraday/auction confirmation and venue-complete trading volume are not secured. A positive gap can reflect active/intermediary turnover; a negative gap can reflect quarterly versus single-day timing or omitted venues. No mechanism story is selected solely by significance.
