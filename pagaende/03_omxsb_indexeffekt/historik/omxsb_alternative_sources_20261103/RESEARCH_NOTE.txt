Alternative data sources — 3 November 2026
Technical source/access check, not thesis text or new economic analysis.

QUESTION
Can another source enable a useful extension after the SHoF fund-flow source
coverage limitation? Yes, distinct source routes remain. No mechanism is now
identified and no additional economic outcome was estimated in this check.

1. NASDAQ HFT / RECONSTRUCTED ORDER BOOK — ACTUAL FORM ACCESS VERIFIED
Public SHoF documentation says the dataset derives from historical ITCH messages
for orders, cancellations and trades at the Stockholm matching engine. It offers
full reconstruction and transaction/second/minute aggregation; attribution only
where available. This is not a guarantee of ultimate fund/client identities or
all-venue coverage.

The root opened the authenticated query form through the user's existing SSE
SSO session. Its displayed date bounds were 2010-02-08 to2026-03-18. Full
reconstruction, transaction and finer time aggregations were selectable. The
form was not submitted; no new extraction or automatic delivery email was
generated in this check. The temporary research tab was closed afterwards.

This verifies access to the form, not complete instrument/date coverage. The
public documentation link to SHoF_NASDAQ_Documentation.pdf returned404. Auction
trade tagging, indicative imbalance fields, correction handling and historical
broker attribution remain unverified. Do not claim the data identifies hedge
funds taking the opposite side, or that a broker ID equals an investor ID.

Possible use: measure stock-specific price/volume behaviour immediately before
the closing auction, at the auction and next session, versus appropriate stocks
and ordinary days. This addresses execution-time absorption or temporary pressure.
It does not separately identify the cause of the earlier announcement premium.
Before a full outcome study, a small fixed source pilot must verify usable auction
records, timestamps, instrument identity and comparison-day coverage.

2. NASDAQ OFFICIAL HISTORICAL INDEX SHARES/WEIGHTS — PRODUCT VERIFIED, ACCESS NOT
Current Nasdaq descriptions identify Global Index Watch as a source of historical
constituents, index weights, Index Shares and corporate actions; proforma coverage
is for selected indexes. OMXSB methodology identifies GIW as a weighting source.
Neither current Nasdaq publication nor the user's Workspace access establishes
our entitlement, exact variant history or recoverable announcement-time vintages.
An old2012 factsheet was inspected but is not used as evidence of current prices
or present service entitlement.

Possible use: verify mechanical changes in required holdings among stocks that
remain members, reducing the confounding from becoming newly included. This was
previously source-blocked, not empirically rejected in the reviewed repo reports.
The prior cost audit had0/78 fully valued before/after baskets; monthly exchange
listed shares are not official index shares. Old/new baskets must be valued at
common prices: price drift is not a required purchase. Common denominator changes
alone may provide no within-review variation in demand per free-float share;
normalizing by volume introduces liquidity variation. Own issuance/float changes
can convey company information. Verified weights therefore enable an initial
variation check, not an automatic causal design. Exact capped/ESG/custom mandates
and pre-announcement tracker capital remain necessary. International incumbent
designs already exist; no novelty claim is made.

3. MODULAR FINANCE HOLDINGS — BROADER OWNERS, OUR ACCESS/HISTORY NOT VERIFIED
The provider describes historical holdings for Nordic issuers and multiple owner
types, including foreign funds and private owners. This could expand observed
ownership beyond the Swedish FI fund population. It would not reveal exact
auction counterparties: dated holdings changes are not transaction-level trades.
Underlying reference dates, nominee transparency, coverage and export access must
be tested. Other universities' access and an old SSE brochure do not certify
Ludvig's current access. No account was created or payment/terms accepted.

FUND-HISTORY REPAIRS ARE SEPARATE
The source reviewer found possible existing vendor-NF windows and exact issuer
dividend documents that could improve selected missing histories. These are not
a verified full repair. Morningstar Direct uses the same underlying vendor and
does not guarantee missing SHoF series exist there; actual student entitlement
is unverified. FI quarter-end holdings or public category-level monthly fund
statistics cannot replace fund-level monthly flow histories.

DECISION
HFT is the first practical source pilot because actual form access is verified.
Official index baskets are a separate potentially valuable acquisition if access
and usable mechanical variation can be demonstrated. Broader owner histories
would improve the ownership question but not settle price causation by themselves.
No new source is sold as a guaranteed decisive finding or prizewinning extension.
