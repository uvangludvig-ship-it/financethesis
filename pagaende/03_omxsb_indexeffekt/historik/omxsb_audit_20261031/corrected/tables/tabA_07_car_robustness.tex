Robustness of Table 2. The first column drops the 27 events of the review announced on 2020-11-10. The second treats every announcement as made after the close, so the announcement day is the next trading day and the announcement and total windows start one trading day later than in Table 2; the third treats every announcement as made before the open, which is the convention of Table 2 (AD is the first trading day on or after the calendar announcement date) and reproduces it. Each cell reports the mean cumulative market-adjusted return (beta = 1) in percent, the HC1 standard error in parentheses, the standard error clustered on announcement date in brackets and the wild cluster bootstrap p-value in braces; the last row of each block is the difference between the last and the second-to-last period. Observations count the events with a total-window CAR over all periods. Stars use the HC1 p-value: * p<0.10, ** p<0.05, *** p<0.01. The wild cluster bootstrap uses 9,999 draws with the null imposed, Webb six-point weights when there are fewer than 12 clusters and Rademacher weights otherwise; the smallest attainable p-value is 2/6\textasciicircum{}G with Webb and 1/2\textasciicircum{}(G$-$1) with Rademacher weights, here G = 9: 2e-07; G = 10: 3.3e-08; G = 18: 7.6e-06; G = 19: 3.8e-06.\end{minipage}
