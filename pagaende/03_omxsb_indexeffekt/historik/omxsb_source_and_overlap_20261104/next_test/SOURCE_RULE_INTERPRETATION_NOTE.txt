No estimator, original lock, source flags or results changed.

The inherited F35 report rule requires a report-metadata row with positive
actual_instrument_rows in all three quarters, a nonblank stable fund ISIN,
and no failed fund-specific unit flag in any of the four frozen stock roles.
The event set must also pass its inherited security/source unit gate.
Absence of the target stock in that supported report is treated as zero.

This verifies report presence/nonemptiness, stable identity and consistency
under the inherited rule. It does not independently certify that every
underlying report is exhaustive, error-free or a complete census of all
economic positions. There are 35 such absence-derived zero endpoints and
zero explicitly reported zero-quantity endpoints in this frozen cohort.

Names such as verified_complete_report_absence in code/private audit fields
and 'complete report' in the historical protocol refer to that inherited
operational source rule. They must not be presented as a new external proof
of report completeness. The reported logical bounds are conditional on the
original cohort, valid measurements and this source convention. They bound
designated missing sources, not every possible reporting error or omission.

The independent replay reconstructed the same source convention from report
metadata and confirmed the arithmetic; it did not establish a stronger
completeness guarantee. Continuous holding remains unidentified even with
perfect endpoint reports.
