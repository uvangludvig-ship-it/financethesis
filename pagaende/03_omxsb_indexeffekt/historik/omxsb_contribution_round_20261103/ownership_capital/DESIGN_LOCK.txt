Existing candidate demand analysis already splits positive changes into zero-pre and existing-pre positions; that is not this planned matched full additive decomposition. Question: in observed FI full-report population, how much of quarterly net position change around additions arises in new versus pre-existing owners? This is a measurement/consequences extension, not a causal or novelty claim.
Primary prospective estimand, if gate passes: review-weighted mean of matched new-owner contribution minus matched pre-existing-owner contribution (continuing + exiting), in percentage points of issuer equity value at common end price. New + continuing + exiting equals total exactly. Event minus mean of its existing fixed three controls. Additions primary; deletions and benchmark subgroups descriptive. One primary contrast and 95% t interval over review means (14 reviews at most), retaining covariance. No significance search or sign-based sample selection; no posthoc thresholds or rematching. Ratios not primary because signed totals can cancel.
Population: retain prior fixed-control ownership ledger and its stable fund ISIN/benchmark, full nonempty reports, instrument alias checks and event-level symmetric common fund support. A fund absent from an instrument becomes zero only in that validated report panel. Gate additionally symmetrically excludes invalid quantity/value/unit fund pairs across all four roles. No absent reports become zero. Fund mergers in a quarter must be excluded symmetrically by fund for every role, including known 51437 in 2022H1.
Quantity and denominator gate precedes outcomes: for each security, q1 minus dated splitfactor*q0, value both sides with one common q1 raw FI median SEK price (>=3 reporting funds agreeing within 1%). Divide by positive company mcap from same historic LSEG measure used in previous validated work, observation at most 7 days old. Never use adjusted shares as raw counts. Existing fully validated event-side gates may be inherited; controls need equivalent endpoint support, dated split evidence for suspicious unit changes, and company-capital feasibility. Quarter price/adjusted-close ratios are diagnostics only, not proof of no action. Unknown factors/identities/capital block the role and hence all four roles. No silent control replacement or manufactured zero. Prefer an exact no-go if equivalent control-capital validation cannot be completed boundedly.
Classification: preserve existing pre-quarter benchmark grouping, held stable. New: q0=0,q1>0, contribution q1; continuing: q0>0,q1>0, contribution q1-factor*q0; exit: q0>0,q1=0, contribution -factor*q0; neither zero. Ownership status uses positive raw quantities. New means newly observed at quarter end, not first-ever investor, transaction or attention.
Limitations: quarterly net positions include subscriptions/redemptions, corporate actions, mergers/transfers, off-market transactions and interim round trips. End price/mcap may be endogenous. Measures one indexed security relative to total issuer equity, not all corporate classes/exposure, economic trades, causal flow, hidden passive AUM or price impact. FI excludes foreign/unreported investors; controls nonrandom and can already be index members. Estimates after many prior analyses; nominal intervals exploratory, not selection-adjusted.
Deliverables: executable local gate and analysis/independent arithmetic, raw input path/hash map, gate/exclusion table, derived aggregate results (no full licensed raw data), compact Swedish takeaway or precise no-go. Any gate amendments must precede outcome inspection and state actual time.
