## Agent's verdicts
- Q6: Cookson et al. 2021 > Goyal-Wahal-Yavuz 2024 > JJM 2016 > Armstrong et al. 2019. The selection leg is well powered; the performance leg is not; no clean Swedish replication unless Morningstar Medalist history is in the master.
- Q7: fold into Q6 as the "connection" variable. No top-journal paper on domestic bias in procurement of financial services was found.
- Q8: Hortacsu-Syverson 2004 > Cooper-Halling-Yang 2021 > Christoffersen-Musto 2002. IO procurement papers supply hypotheses, not tables. No published paper on FTN was found (novelty intact).
- Q9: Sun 2021 > Jayaraman et al. 2002. Exit effects are too fresh to measure well by Dec 2026.

Agent's top 3: (1) Q8 Hortacsu-Syverson plus Cooper-Halling-Yang: replication leg exists on data in hand; extension = the same portfolio priced twice; power high. (2) Q6 Cookson et al. with GWY as design template: selection sharp, performance early evidence only; replication leg hinges on rating history. (3) Q9 Sun 2021: thin.

Editor's note (main session): the data check in file 06 (fact 3) shows only 4 of 49 winners used a PPM-only share class; incumbents kept their ISIN and received a rebate. The "same portfolio priced twice via new classes" extension therefore has almost no variation, and the PPM fee cut is contractual by construction. Q8 is dropped as a thesis; it survives as the benefit side of the cost-benefit (fact 4).
