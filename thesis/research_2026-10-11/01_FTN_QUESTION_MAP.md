# 01. The FTN question map, from first principles

Written 11 October 2026 without reference to any earlier plan. Start from what Fondtorgsnämnden actually does, list every question it raises, check each against the data on disk, the anchor search (files 03 to 05) and the winner pattern (file 02), and score.

## What FTN does, step by step (the institution)

1. Announces a tender for one fund category (e-Avrop), with a scoring rule: quality majority, price minority. Bidders submit a fee and documentation.
2. Qualifies bids, interviews a shortlist, scores, awards. Award decisions and every bidder's name are public (11 rounds, 290 bids, 75 winners).
3. Standstill ten days; contracts about six years; losing incumbents' contracts terminated.
4. Pensionsmyndigheten closes losers to new choices, writes to savers (about three weeks to choose), then on one day redeems 100 per cent of its units in each loser (its stake is 20 to 44 per cent of NAV for the large ones) and moves the capital by default to an "equivalent" winner. New winners are topped up to a common level; incumbent winners keep what they have.
5. PPM fee for the category falls 40 to 70 per cent (the rebate mechanism); the fund's retail fee is untouched.
6. The menu shrinks from about 470 funds to a target of 150 to 200. One round (Global active, 127 bn SEK) is frozen by a court appeal: savers were told, nothing moved.

Every question below is a consequence of one of these steps.

## What the map says

- The institution's unique asset is step 4: a dated, pre-announced, uninformed, very large redemption, repeated across rounds, with a 2019 precedent. Everything that uses step 4 as identification scores high on data, anchor, tutor and winner pattern. Everything that uses steps 2 or 5 (selection, fees, bidding) is either mechanical or has no replication leg. Everything about savers (steps 4 and 6) produces sharp facts but sits outside the tutor's field and the winner distribution.
- Q1 to Q3 are one thesis: the flow is the same event seen at the stock level (price pressure and reversal), the fund level (who paid) and over time (anticipation). Q4 and Q5 supply Table 1: the flow is uninformed (94 per cent default; the appeal round shows that information alone moves nothing) and the benefit side is 178 MSEK a year.
- Q5 to Q7 are a second, cheaper paper on defaults and gender with all data already in hand. Worth noting to Klug; not this thesis.
