Scoop risk: PE arm HIGH (Kärnä & Myers 2024/25 Econ Letters; Olsson & Tåg JF 2025); serial-acquirer vs PE contrast LOW. Must cite/position vs El Ghoul, Gong & Guedhami (RIBF 2024, EJW mechanisms on Orbis), Williamson & Yang (JCF 2021), Hammer et al (JCF 2022), Andersson et al (SBE 2022, Swedish SME investment-cash-flow sensitivity), Chemmanur, Signori & Vismara (JFM 2023).
Identification: selection on expected growth remains → estimate ATT on "the kind of firm that gets bought"; headline = difference-in-DiD across parent types (less contaminated). Accounting confounds are the killer: koncernbidrag (use EBIT before appropriations), transfer pricing/management fees (serial acquirers leave targets alone → testable), intra-group debt replacing bank debt (treat as mechanism), fusion into parent → attrition biased toward integrating strategics (track Bolagsverket fusion codes, report attrition by parent type, Lee bounds, 3-year window). Event year = first full fiscal year post-closing. Use Serrano koncernmoder changes as universe; CapIQ to classify acquirer type. Match rate >70% and report.
Owner age: NOT an instrument (exclusion fails; director≠owner; 3:12/karens tax timing; soft retirement age). Use as heterogeneity: succession-driven subsample (director ≥62, no younger family board member) where seller motive is plausibly exogenous to prospects. Hazard kink at 65 = descriptive Table 1 only.
Framing: "Buy, Decentralise, Grow? Serial Acquirers, Private Equity and the Financing of Swedish Private Firms". H1 financing synergy (both relax constraints; serial via internal capital w/o leverage). H2 governance/integration (decentralised parents show no growth beyond selection). Null = parent type doesn't matter, selection does — interesting given 25-35x compounder valuations.
