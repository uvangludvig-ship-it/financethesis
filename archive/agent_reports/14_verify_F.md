# Verification F (investment screening & SME deals) — key takeaways
Verified: Sweden Lag 2023:560 in force 1 Dec 2023; förordning 2023:624; ALL investors must notify in skyddsvärd verksamhet (7§), only non-EU can be reviewed/prohibited (4§); thresholds 10/20/30/50/65/90% votes; no size threshold; 7 categories (samhällsviktig verksamhet per MCF föreskrifter — NO SNI list, functional definition → crosswalk needed); expanded list 1 Oct 2024 (ICT). ISP notifications: 114 (Dec 2023), 1,261 (2024), 1,987 (2025), 2026 Q1 532, Q2 646; reviews opened 0/26/20; conditions 0/5/1; prohibitions 0/1/2; ~1.5% reviewed; most notifications in electricity/energy; handling <25 working days. EU 5th report: Sweden is the outlier driving EU case counts (3,136 in 2024).
Austria: Investitionskontrollgesetz in force 25 Jul 2020, non-EU/EEA/CH investors, 10/25/50%, MICRO FIRMS EXEMPT (<10 employees & <2m EUR) → within-country discontinuity. Germany: AWV §55a 27 case groups, §56 thresholds from 1 May 2021 (17th amendment); 2020 amendments dates unverified; non-EU. Denmark: Investeringsscreeningsloven in force 1 Jul 2021 (not directly verified, 403); 5 sectors mandatory 10%, cross-sector voluntary 25%; 2024 amendment. Finland: 172/2012 amended 682/2020 (date unverified). Norway: Sikkerhetsloven 2019, 1/3 threshold; 2023 amendment (10/20/33/50/67/90%) in force date UNCLEAR → drop as treatment. Switzerland: adopted Dec 2025, not in force, only state-controlled foreign investors → drop. Iceland: no general law → control.
