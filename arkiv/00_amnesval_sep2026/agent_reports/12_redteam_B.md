# Red team B (electricity zones 2022) — key takeaways
Prior: Bălașa & Pollitt 2026 EPRG WP 2602 (Cambridge, SSE co-affiliation, thanks Örjan Sjöberg): descriptive, no firm-level regressions, explicitly invites firm-level work. Bijnens et al (ECB WP 2021/SSRN 2024): Belgian electricity price × financial constraints → jobs (pre-crisis). Alpino, Citino & Frigo (Banca d'Italia): pass-through, elasticity≈0. No causal firm-level evaluation of any European firm energy-support scheme found. FC-amplification with Swedish zones: NOT done. Elstöd: NOT evaluated (Riksbank/KI/Tillväxtanalys unsearched).
FACTS (Nord Pool annual avg öre/kWh SE1/SE2/SE3/SE4): 2020 15/15/22/27; 2021 43/43/67/82; 2022 63/66/137/162; 2023 46/46/59/80; 2024 29/28/41/57. Gap started H2 2021, vanished Nov–Dec 2022 (Dec 2022: 206/206/269/271). → treatment continuous from mid-2021, NOT a post-2021 dummy.
Elstöd till företag SFS 2023:233: SE3 50 / SE4 79 öre per kWh consumed Oct 2021–Sep 2022; cap 20m SEK per group; grid contract on 17 Nov 2022; application 30 May–25 Sep 2023; via Skatteverket tax account; expected ~29bn SEK (press release Feb 2023); ~600k uttagspunkter. "17.5bn paid" UNVERIFIED. Second overlapping scheme for energy-intensive firms (Energimyndigheten, Oct–Dec 2022, not zone-restricted) must be netted. Elstöd is tax-secret at firm level → sits in "övriga rörelseintäkter"; must be imputed = rate × consumption (collinear with exposure); bunching at cap dead (binds only >25–40 GWh).
Fixed-price share: SCB EN0301 has contract type by zone but no household/business split; firm hedging unobservable → ITT.
Serrano: municipality variable UNVERIFIED; single-unit flag needed.
Red team mitigations: single-unit SMEs; continuous exposure zone price × industry intensity; industry×year + municipality FE; border municipalities; event-study pre-trends 2015–2020; FC×rate-cycle confound → run FC interaction as placebo in SE1/SE2; elstöd chapter = secondary DiD SE4 vs SE3 2023–24 with intensity caveat.
Is it finance: lead with balance-sheet outcomes (cash, net debt, payables, dividends, aktieägartillskott, interest coverage, konkurs) → Fazzari/Almeida tradition; elstöd = cash windfall (Blanchard, López-de-Silanes & Shleifer 1994 JFE). Tutor: Schubert.
Framing: "Powerless? Cash, credit and the 2022 electricity shock in southern Sweden." H1 financing frictions; H2 pass-through/windfall; null = absorbed via prices, 29bn mis-targeted.
Scores: originality 7, identification 5, feasibility 6, prize 6 (8 as finance paper).
