# FTN feasibility, round 1 (four agent reports, 11 Oct 2026, condensed)

Four questions from the synopsis were tested against the data in `financethesis_ny/data`. Descriptives only; no treatment coefficient was opened.

## A. Who moved the money (default vs own choice)
Across 67 losers whose money has moved (7 rounds, SEK 75.6 bn): about 6% of capital left voluntarily between award and the default move, 94% was moved by default; 5.6% of savers chose themselves. Default moves are booked inside handel_netto in 1-5 tranches; the split uses a detected move-start month. Per round: EU active -0.3% voluntary, EU index 4.0%, Global index 12.9%, Nordic active -1.3%, Nordic small 2.2%, SE active 4.8%, SE index 4.9% (7.9% incl. Avanza Zero), EU small 2.0%. Winners' inflows match losers' outflows (SE active: losers -15.7 bn Jan-Feb 2026, six new winners +17.5 bn; SE index winners +40.6 bn vs 38.0 bn out). Caveats: Global index March 2025 ambiguous; December placement; renumbering (abrdn, Nordea Stars, SEB Europe, HB Norden Selektiv, SEB 3); master list stale (to July 2026). Verdict: strong descriptive fact, thin as a main analysis (7 rounds, about 10 losers with real money). Files: `data/agentA/`.

## B. Fees on and off the platform
fee_net (PPM fee after rebate) steps down in the exact month a contract starts, 2-8 months after award. fee_gross (the fund's public TER; matches Morningstar OngoingCharge, corr 0.99) does not move for any of about 20 continuing winners; controls flat. Morningstar FEES.txt has one prospectus date per class, no history. Winners' net fee before/after (12m): EU index 0.122 to 0.054; Global index 0.130 to 0.040; Nordic active 0.274 to 0.200; Nordic small 0.410 to 0.243; SE active 0.200 to 0.120; SE index 0.130 to 0.039. About half the winners are new share classes with no PPM history. Verdict: mechanical PPM effect, clean null on spillover; a descriptive section, not a design. Files: `data/ftn_category_fees.csv`, `data/winners_fee_series.csv`.

## C. Non-PPM flows of winners vs losers
PPM to Morningstar crosswalk: 512 of 514 funds (99.6% by count and capital). Only 269 of 511 funds are SEK-denominated (85.7% of PPM capital); no FX file, so flows computed for SEK funds only. Usable rounds R1-R4: 23 winners (18 SEK), 65 losers (38 SEK); controls 49-124 SEK funds per round; post-award months to Aug 2026: 29, 22, 18, 12. Mean monthly non-PPM flow pre vs post (pooled R1-R4): winners -0.02 to +1.20%, losers -0.12 to +0.20%, controls -0.16 to -0.28%; an alternative measure (Morningstar net flow minus PPM trade) flips the winner result (correlation 0.40). Losers' fate: all 66 still "Active" in the Jan 2026 fundmaster snapshot; TNA series stop for 5 (abrdn Europe ex UK, Odin Europa, Templeton European Opportunities, Aktie-Ansvar Sverige, Lannebo Sverige Hallbar); median return-adjusted TNA change for 35 SEK losers -1.8%. Verdict: too few treated funds and two measures disagree; exploratory only. Files: `data/crosswalk_ppm_shof.csv`.

## D. Bid-level dataset
All 11 rounds parsed: 290 bids, 75 winners, 109 incumbents (old build said 108), 34 new-to-platform winners, 217 qualified bids. Bid deadlines extracted from each report's timeline. 259 of 290 bids match a Morningstar fund; 12-month returns exist for 146 bids (32 of 176 non-PPM bidders); 144 bids lack returns, listed in `data/unmatched_for_lseg.csv`. Raw means within round: 12-month return percentile winners 0.59 vs losers 0.50; 36-month 0.61 vs 0.49; size percentile 0.66 vs 0.46; median size 15.6 bn vs 7.6 bn; PPM net fee (incumbents) 0.24% vs 0.33%; incumbents win 37% vs 19%; Swedish managers 47% vs 14%. Post-award return months for winners: EU active 30, Global index 19, EU index 22, Nordic 18-19, SE rounds about 13, Global active 3, small caps 4, Tech 0. Verdict: feasible as descriptive and secondary; MDE on return percentile about 14 points vs an observed 8-12 point gap; incumbency, Swedish manager and size are confounded. Files: `data/bids_long.csv`, `data/coverage_by_round.csv`, `data/winner_loser_means_by_round.csv`.
