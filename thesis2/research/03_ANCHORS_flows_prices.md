## Swedish swing pricing: what is verified
- Legal since **1 July 2023**: prop. 2022/23:65 "Ett likviditetsverktyg for fonder" (SFS 2023:234); FI permission required (applications from 1 May 2023). FI beslutspromemoria dnr 22-25593 amended FFFS 2013:9 (vardepappersfonder) and 2013:10 (specialfonder): ch. 7 s. 8a written routines; ch. 23 s. 12a fund rules must state method and maximum justeringsfaktor; ch. 29 s. 17 p. 6 prospectus worked example; **ch. 31 s. 48b annual and half-year reports must state the factor actually applied in the period** (a data source). Threshold need not be disclosed. Sources: fi.se beslutspm-fs2310-2311.pdf; regeringen.se prop. 2022/23:65.
- Pensionsmyndigheten's remissvar (regeringen.se, 2022) supports the tool and asks that justerat fondandelsvarde be made **mandatory** on days when justified.
- Adoption: SEB Investment Management, 41 funds incl. SEB Sverigefond and SEB Global Aktiefond, from **14 March 2025**, max factor **1 per cent** (sebgroup.com kundunderrattelse). Handelsbanken, Robur, Nordea, LF, Skandia, Lannebo, Didner & Gerge and others: NOT VERIFIED; check fund by fund in fondbestammelser. Before 2023 Swedish-domiciled funds could not swing; Luxembourg and Irish UCITS on the platform could, so the 2019 purge gives domicile variation (NOT VERIFIED per fund). Whether the PPM fondavtal bans entry or exit fees and how it treats a swung NAV: NOT VERIFIED.
- FTN reports publish winners and fees but score charts only as images; an RD on bid score is NOT VERIFIED as feasible.

## Agent's overall top 3 for an 8-week BSc thesis

1. **Q3, anchor Jin et al. (2022) RFS, with Edelen (1999) for the dilution measure.** Replicate the swing-pricing DiD on Swedish adoptions (daily Morningstar flows, SEB 14 Mar 2025 confirmed, others to be found in fondbestammelser), then use FTN exit days as the sharpest large-redemption events in any dataset: PM stake 20-44 per cent of NAV, date known, no strategic motive. Index losers give almost noiseless power (tracking error 3-5 bp a day vs 10-20 bp expected dilution, about 100 fund-tranche days), and the annual-report disclosure of the applied justeringsfaktor identifies who swung. Policy punchline: Pensionsmyndigheten asked for mandatory swing pricing in 2022, then imposed the redemptions. Risk: few losers had FI permission; mitigate with the 2019 purge and foreign-domiciled funds.
2. **Q1+Q2, anchor Lou (2012) RFS with Wardlaw (2020) and Greenwood-Sammon (2025) JF.** Replicate FIT on the Swedish fund universe, then treat FTN forced net sales and purchases as a price-independent FIT shock, with three dated windows (award, letter, execution) for anticipation, and the FI short-position register for front-running. Tutor fit highest. Risk: managers trade between award and exit so exit-day CARs understate; use quarterly holdings changes and the small-cap wave (Oct 2026) where per-stock pressure relative to ADV is largest.
3. **Q5, anchor Pavlova-Sikorskaya (2023) RFS with Berk-van Binsbergen (2025) JFE as the bound.** The state moved about 38 bn (eventually 65 bn) from OMXS30/SBX trackers to ESG-screened trackers, a permanent BMI shock for a handful of excluded names. Ownership effects measurable in FI holdings; the price effect almost surely undetectable, so the contribution is a calibrated bound. Choose only if comfortable defending a precise null. Q4 (McLemore 2019 / PST 2015) is the fallback: easy replication, no power on alpha.
