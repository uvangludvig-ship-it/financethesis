EXPLORATORY FIXED-SECURITY CHECK, 2026-11-02

Purpose: test whether the directional addition/deletion association persists
within the same securities, after removing review-wide conditions and observed
pre-event differences. This addresses only time-invariant security selection.
It does not distinguish demand, attention, anticipation, or time-varying news.
The overall price results and many previous robustness checks are already known.
This is not blind preregistration or a new independent confirmation.

Source: the frozen dated-main-market matched event_paths.csv (287 rows, post2017).
No new control selection or outcome windows. Retain exact RICs observed at both
addition and deletion, rather than merge different A/B classes. Require finite
existing diff3_1, event/control preCAR and log market cap. Determine both-direction
eligibility on that complete data set. Ex post repeated-event selection limits
generalisability and is not a prospective trading filter.

Outcome: original event-minus-three-controls CAR AD-1 through ED+1, percentage
points. Indicator add=1, deletion=0. The coefficient is the addition-minus-deletion
contrast, NOT the causal effect of inclusion. PreCAR and log market-cap imbalance
are event values minus the average of the same three frozen controls.

Primary: equal total weight per review, security and review fixed effects, both
pre-event imbalance controls. Descriptive companion: same sample/fixed effects,
without imbalance controls. Exactly these two specifications, no period, horizon,
cutoff, weight or model search. No substitute sample if results are uninteresting.

Report review-cluster CR1 t(19-1) intervals, issuer-cluster and two-way covariance
as diagnostics with their actual cluster counts; keep negative-variance problems
visible. Primary review-cluster restricted wild bootstrap-t, Rademacher weights,
9999 draws and fixed seed 20261102. Report high-dimensional fixed-effect rank,
leverage, direction counts, all exclusions and within-security variation. Nominal
exploratory inference after extensive previous analyses; no multiplicity-free
claim. A positive result is a robustness check, not a newly identified mechanism.
