EXPLORATORY SAME-SECURITY CHECK — TECHNICAL DECISION NOTE

Question: Does the addition-versus-deletion association survive comparison within
the same share class, controlling review conditions and the existing matching
imbalances? No new controls, return windows or time splits were tried.

Sample: 244 events, 75 securities in 74 issuers, 19 reviews from 2017 onward.
All securities are observed in both directions. This is an ex post switcher
population, not the full index-change population or a prospective strategy.

Primary model: equal-review weights, security/review fixed effects, pre-event
return and log market-cap balance against the same three original controls.
Addition-minus-deletion contrast: +2.37945 percentage points.
Review-cluster 95% interval: [-0.07126, 4.83016].
Two-way issuer/review diagnostic interval: [-0.48597, 5.24486].
Review wild-bootstrap p=0.0433. These are different finite-sample procedures;
one p below .05 does not override the intervals and clustering sensitivity.

Descriptive model without balance covariates: +2.53669 points; review interval
[0.24996, 4.82342], two-way interval [-0.15124, 5.22463].

Interpretation: the direction of the prior association persists when comparing
the same securities across different index changes, but its precision is not
stable across inference methods. This cannot rule out time-varying selection,
firm news, anticipation, or momentum not captured by the measured pre-window.
Maximum primary leverage is 0.832 and there are only 19 review clusters.

Decision: useful supplementary robustness; NOT a new mechanism finding or a
justification for promising a prize-winning contribution. Preserve both models
and all uncertainty. No further windows/cutoffs added in search of significance.

QA: initial percentage-point scaling and historical issuer-label errors were
corrected, with the invalid run preserved and correction log included.
