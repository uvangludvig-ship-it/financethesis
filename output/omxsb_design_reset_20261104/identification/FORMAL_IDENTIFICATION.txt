NO NEW ESTIMATION: observational-equivalence and proposed-source audit only.

1. Measurement is not assignment.
For fund f, a conventional inferred flow is
 Fhat_ft = A_ft - A_f,t-1*(1+R_ft) - merger_transfer_ft.
It requires complete dated shareclass aggregation, consistent currencies,
matching actual valuation dates, correct dividends/splits and transfer rules.
Period-end cash timing is an approximation when flows occur within period.
An accurate F does not by itself become exogenous. Do not mechanically claim
that using fund return makes every correctly measured flow correlated with
stock return; the substantive investor-decision channel remains even with
perfect flow measurement, and imperfect matching adds another problem.

2. The candidate shift-share exposure.
Let w_if,t-1 be a genuinely predetermined portfolio value weight and M_i,t-1
the chosen stock-size normalizer. Let g_ft be externally shifted cash flow.
 Z_it = sum_f w_if,t-1 * g_ft / M_i,t-1.
The proposal currently substitutes ordinary inferred Fhat_ft for g_ft.
No source identifies an external assignment mechanism for that substitution.
Fixed/lower-frequency w prevents looking forward; it does not prove exogeneity.

3. Missing exclusion restriction.
 R_it = beta*D_it + b_i*U_t + eps_it,
 F_ft = a_f*U_t + eta_ft,
 D_it = pi*Z_it + v_it.
U may be Sweden/style news, risk appetite or common investor reallocation.
Then E[Z_it*(b_i*U_t+eps_it)] need not vanish. With no independent exclusion,
for any beta' the residual can be redefined as eps'=eps+(beta-beta')*D;
the same observed prices/flows/holdings fit alternative demand effects.
A fitted first stage or a large F statistic cannot choose among them.
This is a statement about absent maintained restrictions, not a theorem
that no economic model could ever identify demand from this class of data.

4. Tracker geometry, a theoretical diagnostic, not an empirical finding.
For one exactly capitalization-weighted fund, w_i=M_i/sum_j M_j:
  w_i*F/M_i = F/sum_j M_j.
This is constant across members at a date. Date fixed effects remove it.
Market-return controls may remove precisely a common demand component.
Free-float/cap/ESG weighting breaks exact equality, but remaining exposure
variation can load on size, style, liquidity and common fundamentals.
ADV normalization creates different exposures; it does not create random ones.
Using many stock rows cannot manufacture new underlying fund-flow shocks.

5. Timing and first-stage observability.
Quarterly holdings measure signed net endpoint changes, not daily trades.
Daily NAV/TNA residuals do not show cash-to-equity pass-through timing.
If whole quarters containing ordinary June/December implementation and
May/November announcements are removed, Q1/Q3 over2019–2023 give10periods
before other source or mandate restrictions. This is a calendar count,
not an effective sample-size or power calculation. Correlated fund shocks,
serial flow dependence and common holdings further constrain inference.
Do not scan daily lags, smoothing horizons or shock tails for a useful sign.

6. Identification vs transport to index events.
Even valid exogenous-flow identification estimates an effect for the cash
shocks and trading margin that actually moved. Applying that coefficient to
index additions needs comparable execution, anticipation, liquidity and
demand-dose measurement, plus a maintained response function across settings.
An in-sample fit to index CAR does not validate all those assumptions.
Cross-fitting can limit overfitting; it cannot supply exclusion or transport.

7. Minimal reopening condition.
Before any outcome run, write the specific institutional assignment and its
observable date/amount rules, why it changes the fund's cash, why its direct
effect on the selected stocks is excluded, and how other receiving/selling
funds are handled. Then pass exact fund/shareclass/FX/dividend/merger gates.
Predeclare dose, units, lag and one window using execution rules, not returns.
If only an exogenous cash exposure is known, target reduced form. To target
price per executed demand unit, independently measure or validate pass-through.
No numerical forecast threshold is proposed. No result is authorized here.

8. Scope.
Only2019–2023 would be considered for this candidate. No FTN outcomes may
be opened; pre2024 restriction is this candidate's scope, not a redefinition
of PLAN's narrower FTN embargo. No index-event return, q0/q2 capital or flow
outcome was opened by this audit. Existing F18/F20/F31 summaries were read.
