Figure 1. OMXSB index funds: trading at index changes and relative size, 2007–2026. Panel A reports annual mean D for additions (positive bars) and deletions (negative bars); labels give the number of events in each mean. D is index funds’ net buying between the report before AD (announcement date) and the report after ED (effective date), as a percentage of the share class’s shares. Deletion D is sign-adjusted in the source and plotted with its sign reversed. Missing means are omitted. Panel B shows index funds’ assets divided by OMXSB members’ aggregate market value at the final review of the year, in percent. This aggregate asset ratio measures the funds’ relative size; it is not a direct measurement of their holdings in every index constituent. Source: LSEG Lipper and SHoF; authors’ calculations. The fund classification follows data/input/omxsb/tracker_mandates.csv; the figure is rerun when that file is final.
