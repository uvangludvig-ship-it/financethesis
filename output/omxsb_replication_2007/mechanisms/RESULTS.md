The 81-name table of the 2009-05-11 release (effective 2009-06-01) is chained forward and backward through the 39 reviews with the change lists. Keys are LSEG RICs where the event or constituent has one, otherwise the ticker. Inconsistencies found (6): 2011H1: deleted but not a member (NOKIA.ST); 2011H1: deleted but not a member (CDONAB.ST); 2011H2: deleted but not a member (ENQ.ST^L23); 2022H2: deleted but not a member (COREb.ST); 2026H1: deleted but not a member (SAMPO.ST); 2026H1: deleted but not a member (NORION.ST). These are stocks deleted at a review without a recorded addition: Nokia (the known chain gap), and CDON, EnQuest, Corem, Norion and Sampo, which entered the index through corporate actions (spin-offs, mergers, fast entry) that are not in the review lists. Members that stop trading (acquired or delisted) are treated as having left the index on their last price date; they are counted separately (`n_exited_delisted` in `membership_coverage_by_review.csv`).

Coverage: 68 to 109 live members per review; the share of members with a market cap in the panel on AD−1 is 0.82 at worst (2007H2) and 0.89 on average, rising from about 0.82 in 2007 to about 0.93 in 2026 (`membership_coverage_by_review.csv`; the missing names per review are in `membership_missing_mcap_by_review.csv`: Nordea, Tieto and TradeDoubler have no RIC in the panel, Millicom has no market cap, and Skanska B and Tele2 B have no `mcap` field and are filled with close × shares outstanding). The LSEG `mcap` is company-level (identical across share classes: median ratio across A/B pairs 0.000), so each company is counted once in the OMXSB total. The reconstructed total is 0.55 of the top-100 .ST sum at the median review, so the notebook's top-100 proxy overstates the denominator (it also counts dual-class companies twice, since both share classes carry the company-level mcap); the E1 regression with the top-100 size is in `e1_table3_size_top100.csv`.

## E3 Migrations: not replicated

G&S's migration test needs a second index with dedicated passive money on the other side of the move (S&P MidCap 400 ↔ S&P 500). In Sweden the index funds tied to Nasdaq Stockholm indices track OMXS30 (XACT OMXS30, Avanza Zero) and OMXSB (section 5 of `omxsb_notes.md`); there is no Nasdaq mid- or small-cap index change list with dedicated trackers in the repo, and OMXS30 is by construction the 30 most traded stocks, which OMXSB (top 10% by turnover, 70-110 names) also contains. A move between the two therefore cannot generate offsetting selling or buying by the other index's trackers: an OMXSB addition of an OMXS30 member means OMXS30 trackers keep holding, not sell. Checking OMXS30 membership at the announcement date from `omxs30_changes_2005_2026.csv` (status only known for tickers that appear in that list): 1 OMXSB addition of a current OMXS30 member (OMXSB-2015H1-03 Securitas AB ser. B) and 6 OMXSB deletions of OMXS30 members (OMXSB-2007H2-12 Stora Enso Oyj ser. R, OMXSB-2009JAN-14 Vostok Gas Ltd. SDB, OMXSB-2012H1-30 Securitas AB ser. B, OMXSB-2015H2-09 Nokia Corporation, OMXSB-2019H2-12 SSAB AB ser. A, OMXSB-2024H2-12 Kinnevik AB ser. B); none is a migration in the G&S sense. No proxy was built (`e3_omxsb_events_vs_omxs30_membership.csv`).

We have closing quoted bid and ask (relative quoted spread), not effective spreads from trade data, and no implementation-shortfall series (G&S Fig. 5 / HBS Fig. 9 green line): that part is not replicated. The value-weighted relative quoted spread of OMXSB constituents falls from 0.27% (2007) to 0.10% (2016) and 0.08% (2025); across all .ST stocks in the panel from 0.79% to 0.21% (`e2_spread_by_year.csv`, `fig_e2_spread_vs_addition_effect.png`). The correlation of the yearly spread with the yearly mean addition effect is 0.64 (20 years). Stock-days with a relative spread above 20% (21443) are dropped as quote errors.

## E4c: selection variable

Nasdaq's methodology ("OMX Stockholm Benchmark Index Methodology", © 2025, https://indexes.nasdaq.com/docs/Methodology_OMXSB.pdf, fetched) ranks the OMXSPI universe by 12-month SEK turnover, selects the top 10% (minimum 25), then adds by free-float market cap within ICB supersectors to 85% coverage and removes the bottom 30% by turnover; reference dates are the last trading day of April and October and the announcement is after the close on the sixth trading day before the effective date. G&S rank by market cap because S&P selects on it; we rank non-constituents by 6-month SEK turnover (price × volume) ending at the reference date (taken as the last trading day of the month before the announcement month) and, as a second version, by market cap. The universe is the panel, not all of Nasdaq Stockholm, and it grows from about 330 to 700 non-constituents over the window, so percentile ranks are reported next to raw ranks. 14 of the 270 additions in the change lists have no rank (no price data in the window).

## Demand measures

For 2019-2026 the realised net trading by OMXSB trackers (`nt`) is used as in G&S Fig. 3. For 2007-2018 (no FI holdings) the expected demand is tracker AUM × index weight / market cap, which with weight = mcap / total OMXSB mcap collapses to AUM / total OMXSB mcap and is identical for every stock in a review; and AUM × weight / ADV, with ADV the mean of close × volume over the 50 trading days ending 11 days before AD. Tracker AUM: fund-level points (Robur Access Sverige, Nordnet Sverige Index, LF Sverige Index from 2025-06-16) interpolated linearly in time and summed from 2019-01-02; before that, linear interpolation from the notes' summary point (SEK 15.0 bn at 2016-12-31) to the fund sum at 2019-01-02 (SEK 13.2 bn); held flat at SEK 15.0 bn before 2016-12-31 (assumption); interpolated series in `tracker_aum_daily_interpolated.csv`; values at review dates: 2007-06-01 SEK 15.0 bn, 2012-06-01 SEK 15.0 bn, 2016-06-01 SEK 15.0 bn, 2019-06-03 SEK 18.84 bn, 2022-06-01 SEK 39.34 bn, 2025-06-02 SEK 54.53 bn, 2026-06-01 SEK 118.2 bn. Mean expected demand in % of market cap by period: 2007–2012 0.43%, 2013–2017 0.28%, 2018–2020 0.31%, 2021–2023 0.37%, 2024–2026 0.60%. The Nov 2010 Nasdaq OMX release's 'about SEK 160 bn tracking the index' is a different concept (benchmarked assets) and is not used.

## Problems flagged

- Universe: the LSEG panel holds OMXSB constituents 2013-2026, the FTN losers' holdings and the mapped 2007-2012 constituents (about 330 to 800 .ST stocks with prices per year), not all of Nasdaq Stockholm. Everything that requires 'all non-constituents' or 'all listed shares' (E1 market turnover, E2 panel spread, E4c ranks) uses this panel.
- Membership chain: six deletions without a recorded addition (Nokia plus five corporate-action entries), the five reviews Jan 2005 to Jan 2007 are missing so the backward chain stops at the July 2007 review, and corporate-action exits are detected only through the last price date. Coverage of the OMXSB market-cap total is 82% to 93% of members.
- `shares` is sparse before 2009 (2% of stock-days 2004-2006, 70% in 2007), so turnover is missing for 21 events and the step-1 share-jump flag cannot be evaluated for 10 deletions. 84 of 136144 stock-months with monthly turnover above 300% of shares outstanding (a unit mismatch between LSEG-adjusted volume and shares, e.g. Cloetta 2008-12) are dropped before the cap-weighted market average; without this cap the market average is dominated by one stock in 2008-11.
- E1 N: 229 of 261 additions and 207 of 226 deletions have all three controls.
- E2: closing quoted spreads, not effective spreads; no implementation shortfall. G&S's figure is descriptive; the regression row is ours.
- E3: no migration test is possible (see above).
- E4b is anchored at ED−1 rather than at AD−1 as in G&S Fig. 9 (the notebook's convention).
- E4c: OMXSB selects on turnover, G&S on market cap; the reference date before 2025 is assumed to be the last trading day of the month before the announcement month; 14 additions have no rank.
- E5b and Fig. 3: FI fund holdings exist only from 2018Q4, so 2007-2018 has no realised net trading and no 13F-type institutional ownership; FI covers Swedish mutual funds, not all institutions.
- Expected demand 2007-2018 rests on a hand-built AUM series: fund points only from 2016 (Nordnet) and 2018 (Robur), the notes' 'roughly SEK 15 bn in 2016' before that, and a flat SEK 15 bn assumption for 2007-2016; XACT OMXSB's AUM was not retrieved. The %-of-mcap measure varies only across reviews (23 clusters in 2007-2018), so its slope is a time-series regression with few effective observations.
- The JF version could not be fetched (HTTP 403); the HBS working paper was used for the later conventions (demeaned controls, extra controls WZ and analyst coverage, which we cannot build).
- One deletion in the events file (226) is not in `deletion_reasons_2007_2026.csv` (225); it is treated as review_only.
