The calendar multiplier procedure concerns sampling uncertainty in estimated
historical flow risk, conditional on ownership weights. It does not account for
the sampling uncertainty of a general index-inclusion effect. The suggested
earlier-quarter placebo and numerical tolerance are proposals for root review,
not approved amendments and not new completed empirical results.

Important limitation: four approximately independent blocks per 24-month window
cannot be turned into reliable 95% coverage by naming a bootstrap. Calibration
and interpretation determine whether any interval can be reported as such.
