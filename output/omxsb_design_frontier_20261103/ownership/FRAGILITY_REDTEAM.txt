Verdict: this is a more economically discriminating question than the SEB comparison, conditional on measurement and support. Proceed to a bounded SOURCE/BASELINE gate, not a promised important finding. It asks whether the composition of the observed domestic-fund capital becomes more or less exposed to correlated investor withdrawals, despite an increased owner count. That distinguishes capital diversification from a list of new names. However, it remains a model-implied exposure, not observed realized price risk, forced sales, a causal effect, or the risk of all shareholders.

Closest prior is direct, not distant:
Greenwood–Thesmar (2011), Stock Price Fragility, footnote 15 p.481, explicitly reports no systematic fragility change around S&P500 additions and suggests ownership growth and flow correlations can offset. Their Eq.14 pp.475–476 already separates scale, concentration and covariance; Eq.11 warns about unobserved owners. Their flow implementation uses AUM-scaled percentage-flow covariance; their estimator/window is not our proposed 24 monthly observations. Thus neither the index-inclusion question nor the decomposition is new. The additional answer would be whether the Swedish matched change in composition, at a fixed pre-announcement flow-risk distribution, diversifies covered capital and whether scale can offset that change. A null with a useful interval would be informative; a wide null would not resolve the issue. No claim to first international evidence is warranted.

Algebra and units — mandatory before economics:
Let v_j be fund j's common-price value in the stock. Let M be company market capitalisation, h_j=v_j/M, H=sum_j h_j and s_j=h_j/H=v_j/sum_j v_j, over exactly the covered funds. Let f_jm be estimated net currency flow in month m DIVIDED BY LAGGED portfolio AUM, in decimal units. Sigma=cov(f_m), fixed using 24 complete months before the announcement. Then C=s' Sigma s is monthly variance of a pro-rata net flow shock per unit of covered fund capital; G=h' Sigma h=H^2 C is the corresponding market-cap-scaled variance. A sample covariance can be singular; no inverse is needed. Independent checksum: C equals sample variance across pre-announcement months of z_m=sum_j s_j f_jm. Raw currency-flow covariance cannot be substituted into this formula. Company shares in percentage points must not be confused with decimal shares.
At fixed Sigma, C changes only with the covered ownership weights. It does not mechanically rise with H; adding a tiny owner does not automatically produce diversification. Keeping both C and G prevents a composition improvement being sold as lower total stock-level exposure. For a transparent arithmetic supplement, the symmetric exact decomposition is:
G1-G0 = 0.5*(C1+C0)*(H1^2-H0^2) + 0.5*(H1^2+H0^2)*(C1-C0).
This is an identity, not an additional test. New-owner block terms also include cross-covariance with continuing owners; assigning them only their own squared term would omit the essential mechanism. No need to turn that into multiple subgroup tests.

ONE proposed estimand, subject to root review:
Pre-2024 additions only; fixed original three controls; each matched set uses the same covered fund IDs and same covariance matrix for all four stocks at q0 and q1. For each event, compare C1-C0 for event stock with the mean change for its three controls. Average within review, then equally across reviews. This estimates a matched change in the composition of observed fund capital under frozen historical flow risk. Stock/fund selection and the controls' index status are not randomized. Do not label controls all almost-selected or independently outside-index if the membership chain is incomplete.
Do not pick C versus sqrt(C), a log ratio, G, or a benchmark subgroup after observing signs. C can be primary and G/scale arithmetic companion descriptions. The size threshold for a useful interval must be agreed before estimates; for example, a chosen proportional variance reduction has a square-root translation into shock standard deviation, but a round percentage is not automatically economically justified. No numerical threshold or power result is approved in this note.

Gates that can stop the candidate before outcomes:
1. Vendor definition and portfolio identity. Confirm exactly whether the field is observed/estimated net cash flow, monthly period or rolling total, currency/scale, before/after distributions, and whether it is share-class or portfolio-level. Aggregate actual disjoint classes; never repeat total-fund flows across classes. Match FI institutional fund ID and dated ISIN to the portfolio; merger/relabelled predecessor histories need a dated bridge. No missing history filled with zero. I have NOT verified the vendor field documentation, identity coverage or flow files in this subtask.
2. Pre-announcement information. All 24 months, lagged AUM denominators, covariance estimation and any outlier/merger rule must precede announcement, not merely implementation or quarter end. Freeze Sigma for q0/q1 and all controls. Flow-history availability is distinct from availability to an investor in real time; disclose vendor revisions. Pre-2024 sample also means no 2024–25 outcome fallback if power fails.
3. Common reporting and coverage. Require actual full FI reports at q0/q1; stable securities and split factors; a valid absence may be zero, an absent report may not. Define covered funds from report/flow eligibility, not positive ownership. The same fund set must apply to event and all three controls, before and after. If H=0 for any required stock/date, the normalized measure is undefined: exclude the whole matched set, never substitute zero. Report fund-count and capital coverage before/after and by review. History requirements can selectively exclude newly launched funds and miss the very new capital of interest.
4. Covariance reliability. Twenty-four observations for many funds are enough to compute a PSD sample quadratic form but not enough to estimate many noisy pairwise correlations precisely. Use the same complete months; pairwise deletion can create non-PSD matrices. Do not independently bootstrap every covariance entry. Any prespecified resampling must retain the month-wide cross-fund shock vector, serial blocks and overlapping review windows. Shrinkage changes the target toward its chosen correlation structure: lock it before results if used, do not choose whichever yields an effect. A design/power check must propagate covariance estimation uncertainty, not treat Sigma as known.
5. Interpretation of estimated flows. Estimated net flows can contain return-adjustment, FX, distributions and corporate-action errors. Fund flows need not be exogenous liquidity shocks. Funds can buffer flows with cash/derivatives and choose what to trade. The metric assumes proportional stock exposure to investor flows; it is not actual execution or a proven price consequence. Normalizing within Swedish FI funds does not make them representative of foreign funds, institutions, households or the entire ownership base.
6. Effective power. Before 2024 there are at most ten semiannual reviews from 2019–23, fewer after gates. Thousands of funds or covariance cells do not increase the number of index-review shocks. At minimum retain review-level dependence and common controls; repeated funds/stocks and overlapping 24-month windows imply cross-review dependence too. A ten-cluster t interval alone cannot certify precision. Gate feasibility with a precommitted small-sample method and meaningful threshold, using baseline/placebo data only after design approval. Do not simply reuse the old owner-count power result or call this powered because C is continuous.
7. Incremental contribution. A result entirely driven by ownership scale, coverage migration or a single fund is not the proposed composition finding. Locked diagnostics should identify those sources without choosing favorable exclusions. No search across alternative windows, vendors, covariance shrinkages or subgroup definitions after outcomes. If source coverage or precision fails, stop and retain the descriptive capital-vs-count result already completed.

Decision guidance: this candidate passes an IMPORTANCE gate as a focused measured-composition question: do additional names diversify the investor-flow exposure of the capital actually observed? It has not passed DATA, IDENTIFICATION-AS-CAUSAL, or POWER gates. The causal gate is not required for a careful descriptive composition contribution, but the wording must remain descriptive. Passing source and power gates could support a useful thesis extension anchored to the explicit prior null. It cannot currently be sold as resolving why Swedish index price effects changed, proving price fragility changed, or explaining Greenwood–Thesmar's US null.
