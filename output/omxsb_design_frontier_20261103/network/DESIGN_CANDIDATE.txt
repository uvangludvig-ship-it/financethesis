New economic question; the former pre-existing-owner design remains failed.

Question: Does an index entrant acquire selective return association with the
portfolio of its predetermined potential benchmark clientele, beyond association
with other broad-Sweden fund portfolios and changes in the same fixed controls?

Potential clientele is all eligible baseline funds with a simple OMXSB-family
benchmark, whether or not they already hold the entrant. Comparison: eligible
funds with a simple other broad-Sweden benchmark. Classification is q0 only;
neither subsequent purchases nor future benchmark/survival determine inclusion.
This is not a certified actual-buyer set. Historical reference date is before
announcement, but revised FI archive publication dates can be later: do not
claim the exact report versions were publicly available ex ante.

Let V_fj,0 be valid baseline peer capital. For each matched quartet e, define a
COMMON eligible peer set J_e excluding all four issuers, the union of all four
prior industries, and the declared overlapping or focal-review index events.
The two frozen portfolio weights are

  w_gje = sum_{f in F_g} V_fj,0 / sum_{k in J_e,f in F_g} V_fk,0.

Use these same weights for the entrant AND each of the existing three controls,
in both periods. No stock-dependent renormalization except the same observable
return-coverage mask applied to the common portfolios. The constructed return
is a fixed-weight portfolio; it is not the realized return of funds that can
trade, change weights, hold derivatives or receive flows.

Primary descriptive statistic, if separately approved:
  D_e = [(rho_iF,post-rho_iF,pre)-(rho_iO,post-rho_iO,pre)]
      - mean_{c=1,2,3}[(rho_cF,post-rho_cF,pre)
                      -(rho_cO,post-rho_cO,pre)].

ONE window: AD-120calendar days through AD-1; ED+1 through ED+120calendar days.

At least75 jointly valid days in each window for all4stocks and bothportfolios;

Partial correlations use separate linear projections of stock and portfolio

This addresses common-market lead/lag exposure only. It is NOT proof that all
nonsynchronous peer trading or fundamental style changes have been removed.

Report the two group correlations and their pre/post changes, rather than a
simultaneous two-group beta interpreted as a channel share. Primary aggregate
is equal events withinreview, then equal review weight. Effective broad shock

Review-level tCI is exploratory. Reused issuers, controls and peers plus common
calendar shocks limit inference; any future calibration must preserve these

Meaningful precision target: chosen95%CIhalfwidth<=0.05correlation units, seeking
to distinguish changes of0.10. For equal-volatility assets with rho=0.20, a

Precise interpretation: a selective association with a frozen potential-clientele
portfolio. No causal risk effect, no demonstrated nonfundamental comovement,
no causal mediation from ownership change to returns, and no instrument for
actual post-entry purchases. The samepattern can arise from changing unobserved
style/fundamental exposure that also predicts inclusion and fundholdings.

Why the nonholder alternative is not automatic novelty:

Certified report zeros and a separate proposal are needed; no futurepurchaser

Main-contribution recommendation: do not promote this to the final central
contribution on current evidence. It has a defensible descriptive estimand,
but has not yet supplied investor-specific identification beyond conventional
index/style comovement or demonstrated adequate precision. A narrowly scoped
precision feasibility stage is the only justified next execution, and only
after rootreview; another event-outcome search is not justified.
